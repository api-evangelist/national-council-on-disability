# National Council on Disability

> The National Council on Disability (NCD) is an independent US federal agency established in 1978 that advises the President, Congress, and federal agencies on disability policy. NCD publishes policy reports, congressional testimony, stakeholder letters, progress reports, toolkits, and accountability documents covering civil rights, health care, employment, housing, transportation, education, emergency management, technology, and international disability policy.

## How to retrieve NCD content

- [Sitemap](https://www.ncd.gov/sitemap.xml): XML sitemap, 1,827 URLs — the complete machine-readable index of the site and the only structured enumeration NCD publishes.
- [Atom feed](https://www.ncd.gov/feed.xml): Jekyll-generated Atom 1.0 feed of newsroom posts, letters, reports and meeting announcements. The only dated, parseable NCD output stream.
- [robots.txt](https://www.ncd.gov/robots.txt): declares the sitemap only; no Disallow, no Crawl-delay, no agent-specific rules. Crawling is unrestricted.

## Security and accessibility

- [Vulnerability disclosure policy](https://www.ncd.gov/accountability/vulnerability-disclosure-policy/): v1.0, issued 2021-06-01. Safe harbor for good-faith research, 90-day researcher embargo, 3-business-day acknowledgement, anonymous reports accepted. Caveat for agents and scanners: the reporting address printed in the policy is the unreplaced CISA template placeholder `security@agency.gov`, and there is no /.well-known/security.txt on either host (404, probed 2026-09-14). Use the [contact page](https://www.ncd.gov/contact/) instead.
- [Accessibility](https://www.ncd.gov/accessibility/): NCD states its pages are designed to meet or exceed Section 508 (29 U.S.C. 794d) and conform to WCAG 2.0 Level AA. Section 508 coordinator contact is published on that page.
